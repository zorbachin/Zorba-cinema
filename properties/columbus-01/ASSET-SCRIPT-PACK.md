# COLUMBUS 01 — Production Asset Script Pack

Status: DRAFT PRODUCTION INSTRUCTIONS
Project relationship: GENERATED_CONCEPT / portfolio demonstration
Canonical repo: zorbachin/Zorba-cinema

## Truth boundary
COLUMBUS 01 is a concept, not an actual address/listing/client. No street number, agent, brokerage, MLS, school district, commute time, price, square footage or neighborhood claim may be invented. The purpose is to prove the same cinematic-listing engine can feel credible for a mainstream upper-end Central Ohio home.

## Locked property identity
Generate ONE repeatable property identity before producing room variants:
- contemporary-traditional Central Ohio home
- two stories plus modest roofline, not a mega-mansion
- warm red-brown brick with limited pale limestone accents
- painted warm-white/soft-greige siding on secondary volumes
- natural warm oak front door
- black/dark bronze window frames
- practical window sizes
- medium-pitch charcoal roof
- attached garage kept visually secondary
- mature deciduous trees, clipped hedges, lawn and restrained perennial beds
- no palms, desert planting, ocean, mountains or resort cues
- interiors: light oak floors, warm-white walls, restrained black accents, real family-scale furniture
- daylight moving into early evening

Continuity sentence for manifests:
**Warm brick, pale stone accents, oak entry, charcoal roof and dark-framed practical windows are locked across every exterior and interior view.**

## Production method
First create/approve 01-exterior as the property identity keyframe. Every subsequent frame must use that keyframe as the house reference. If the tool cannot preserve identity, stop rather than generating unrelated luxury-home images.

All masters:
- 16:9 or wider, minimum 2560px long edge where available
- editorial real-estate photography, not hyper-luxury advertising
- no baked text, addresses, signs, logos or watermarks
- human presence optional but generally avoid people so the listing remains property-led

## Frame scripts

### 01-exterior
Purpose: street-facing hero and identity lock.
Frame: believable affluent Columbus-area residential street, late-morning/afternoon soft light. Two-story contemporary-traditional home using the locked brick/stone/siding palette. Mature deciduous landscaping. Camera around 35mm-equivalent, slightly off-center, natural verticals.
Reject: California modern box, Florida villa, Scottsdale compound, oversized gates.

### 02-entry
Purpose: move from street to threshold.
Frame: SAME oak front door, same brick/stone arrangement and window geometry visible from closer approach.
Camera: eye-level 28–35mm, enough doorway area for a mask transition.
Match requirement: a buyer must recognize this as the same facade as frame 01.

### 03-great-room
Purpose: strongest interior reveal.
Frame: light oak floor, warm-white walls, dark-framed practical windows, subtle fireplace or built-in if architecturally plausible, family-scale seating. Bright but not overexposed.
Reject: double-height resort atrium, wall of ocean glass, nightclub lighting.

### 04-kitchen
Purpose: useful high-end residential detail.
Frame: connected open-plan kitchen with warm oak/painted cabinetry, stone counters, practical island, same floors and trim as great room.
Reject: showroom fantasy or a materially different house style.

### 05-primary-suite
Purpose: calm private room.
Frame: generous but believable bedroom, same window trim/flooring/material level, layered neutral textiles.
Reject: hotel staging, panoramic coastal glass.

### 06-primary-bath
Purpose: finish-quality proof.
Frame: restrained stone/tile, double vanity, glass shower and/or tub only if plausible, same black/bronze hardware family.
Reject: spa resort aesthetic.

### 07-backyard-patio
Purpose: connect interior to exterior and prove identity.
Frame: same rear massing, brick/siding/windows consistent with front, usable patio, lawn and deciduous planting. Optional modest firepit/seating.
Reject: infinity pool, palms, desert hardscape, enormous resort kitchen.

### 08-floorplan-placeholder
Purpose: engine/demo slot, not fake property data.
Do NOT generate a plan with invented room labels or measurements. Use a neutral abstract architectural-plan texture or UI placeholder clearly labeled by the SITE as “Floor plan supplied with a real listing.” No measurements baked into media.

### 09-neighborhood
Purpose: Central Ohio context without fabricated claims.
Frame: tasteful tree-lined residential context / neighborhood street / greenway-like environment consistent with Columbus-area visual language. No business names, school logos, recognizable claimed district or commute times.
Site copy:
Title: Context, when it is real.
Body: A client version uses verified neighborhood and listing data supplied by the agent.

### 10-evening-exterior
Purpose: close on the SAME house.
Frame: exact house from 01 at blue hour, warm interior light, modest landscape lighting, same driveway, door, windows, roof and planting.
Reject if any facade geometry changes.

## Required crop
Create one 4:5 crop-safe hero from 01-exterior with the front door and primary facade inside the central 60% width.

## Contact-sheet acceptance
Review 01–10 together. Reject the pack if:
- front/rear massing no longer feels related
- door or window arrangement changes materially
- region reads California/Florida/Arizona
- materials drift
- rooms look like different houses
- invented listing facts appear
- any frame contains text/signage that could be mistaken for real property data
