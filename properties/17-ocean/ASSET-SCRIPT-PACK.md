## Locked property identity
The approved visual boards establish the house identity:
- coastal warm-modern architecture
- pale limestone / warm mineral plaster
- warm oak / walnut-toned millwork
- dark slim-framed floor-to-ceiling glazing
- broad ocean-facing openings
- quiet, low-slung luxury scale rather than a resort compound
- stone threshold / courtyard entry with a rectangular ocean sightline
- warm neutral interiors with dark stone accents
- restrained Mediterranean/coastal planting
- sunset moving into blue hour
- Pacific-coast emotional treatment

Continuity sentence for manifests:
**Pale limestone, warm wood, dark-framed glazing and the same ocean-facing threshold axis are locked across every frame.**

## Production method
Use the supplied storyboard as the image-reference authority. The goal is to create clean production frames that look like the SAME property shown there, without any baked-in UI or typography.

For every frame:
- 16:9 or wider master, minimum 2560px long edge where available
- no words or interface elements in the image
- no visible address numbers
- no people unless specifically requested below
- architectural geometry must match the reference house
- preserve coast orientation and sunset direction
- do not introduce new facade materials or a different window system
- reject any result that looks like a different house
