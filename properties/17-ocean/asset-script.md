## Identity lock

Use the later Santa Monica board as the visual identity anchor.

Locked:
- two-story warm coastal modern residence
- pale limestone/plaster exterior
- warm oak interior planes
- charcoal/dark metal window frames
- broad rectangular glazing, not arched or resort-style
- pale warm stone floors throughout
- high flat ceilings, minimal recessed lighting
- restrained olive-like/dry-climate landscape
- west-facing Pacific horizon
- low neutral furniture, warm gray/sand upholstery
- no pool as the hero device
- no tropical palms as the dominant cue
- no Mediterranean villa arches
- no glossy all-white marble hotel aesthetic

## Production rule

Every final must look like a new camera position in the same property. If the facade, glazing module, floor tone, wood species, ceiling height or coast orientation changes materially, reject it.

## Required finals and shot direction

1. 01-arrival-coast.webp
Camera: very wide aerial/telephoto coastal establishing frame, property readable but not dominant.
Time: late golden hour.
Narrative job: emotion and geography before product.
Avoid: fake skyline labels, fake street number, dramatic resort pool.

2. 02-approach-exterior.webp
Camera: human-height 28–35mm equivalent on the entry path.
Continuity: same facade massing and landscape from 01.
Narrative job: create a readable path to door.

3. 03-threshold-door.webp
Camera: centered or slightly off-center 28mm at the open threshold.
Continuity: same dark wood door, pale stone floor and exterior wall texture.
Narrative job: doorway is a clean portal mask into the next scene.

4. 04-living-hero.webp
Camera: 24–28mm from rear/side of living room, not ultra-wide distortion.
Continuity: dark-framed glazing, pale stone, warm oak, low neutral furniture.
Narrative job: primary emotional reveal.

5. 05-kitchen.webp
Camera: 28–35mm, readable island and circulation.
Continuity: same floor/ceiling/glazing, warm oak + charcoal stone or restrained dark stone island.
Narrative job: practical luxury, not showroom fantasy.

6. 06-primary-suite.webp
Camera: 35mm, calmer composition.
Continuity: same glazing module and coastal orientation.
Narrative job: quieter version of the same architectural language.

7. 07-bath-wellness.webp
Camera: 28–35mm.
Continuity: pale limestone, warm oak, restrained metal fixtures.
Narrative job: wellness without becoming a different resort/spa project.

8. 08-terrace.webp
Camera: 28mm from terrace edge back toward house and coast.
Continuity: living-room glazing and exterior massing should be visible.
Narrative job: prove inside/outside spatial connection.

9. 09-location.webp
Camera: broad Santa Monica/Pacific coast context, no map labels baked in.
Narrative job: location mood only.
No fabricated distance, commute, business, school or neighborhood data.

10. 10-night-exterior.webp
Camera: same angle family as 02 or a slightly wider version.
Time: blue hour.
Continuity: exact same facade, entry and glazing.
Narrative job: closure and proof the property stayed the same.
